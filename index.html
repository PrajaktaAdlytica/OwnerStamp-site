Questions, answered

Clear before
you begin.

Does OwnerStamp replace our identity provider?

No. The initial direction is a read-only reconciliation and attestation overlay. Identity issuance and access changes remain with the source systems.

Which systems are included in the first evaluation?

The proposed scope is one Entra export, one AWS IAM export, and one approved agent manifest. Exact formats, permissions, and handling are agreed before an evaluation.

Does the demo change real access?

No. Every record is synthetic. Assignments, expiry states, and proposed revocation drafts remain local to the demo and reset without external actions.

Can we request a demo separately?

Yes. A guided walkthrough uses synthetic records and has its own request page. It is separate from the redacted inventory review and paid evaluation.
