Confidence is part of the record

Know what the
record can support.

Preserve the distinction between a direct source reference, an inferred relationship, and an unconfirmed ownership gap.

Source-linked

A direct reference.

A relationship supported by an available record reference. Coverage can still be incomplete.

Inferred

A candidate match.

A relationship proposed from the extracts that still needs human confirmation.

Unattributed

A visible gap.

No confirmed owner or supported edge. Keep it in the reviewer’s queue.
