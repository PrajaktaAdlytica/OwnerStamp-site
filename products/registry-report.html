Questions, answered

Clear before
you begin.

Is Registry Report a live product?

It is a proposed module direction. The first evaluation is a bounded reconciliation and ownership-review workflow, with scope agreed before any customer extract is transferred.

What is outside the first evaluation?

Live credential issuance, universal discovery, runtime enforcement, and automatic revocation are excluded. Source availability and exact formats must be scoped.

Can I see the record workflow?

Yes. The synthetic demo includes Inventory, Graph, Attestations, and Evidence views. Every transition stays inside the browser and can be reset.
